How it works

Three steps to your
perfect team.

CoMatch streamlines team formation — from finding the right community Space, to posting your needs, to locking in your dream collaborators.

01
🏠

Find Your Space

Join or create a Space dedicated to your project, competition, or idea. Each Space is a focused community of builders.

02
📣

Post a Call

Share your open roles — UI Designer, Backend Engineer, Product Manager — and the skills you need. Filled fast.

03
🤝

Connect & Conquer

Chat with potential teammates, align on goals, build trust — then officially form your team and get building.

Features

Everything you need
to team up.

Five powerful features designed to make finding great collaborators feel effortless.

🔍

Smart Spaces

Each Space is dedicated to a single project or competition. Browse posts, recruit the best teammates, or join teams that match your vibe.

📋

Structured Calls

Post teammate calls with a title, description, open roles, and skill requirements. Attach a preview image to stand out in the Space feed.

👤

Profile as Resume

Your CoMatch profile is your professional identity. Add skills, experiences, and achievements — then link LinkedIn so teammates can verify your background before accepting you.

  • Showcase your proudest achievements
  • List skills, tools, and past projects
  • LinkedIn verification for trust
  • Let your work speak before you say a word
